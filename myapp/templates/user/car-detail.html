Historical Significance

The 1965 Ford Mustang, often referred to as the 1964½ model, launched the "pony car" class of American automobiles and created a sensation in the automotive world. This particular example represents the early production with its unique features that distinguish it from later 1965 models.

With its long hood, short deck design, and affordable price point, the Mustang appealed to a broad demographic and sold over one million units in its first 18 months of production. The 289 V8 engine provided spirited performance while maintaining reliability for daily driving.

This car has been part of a private collection for the past 15 years, stored in a climate-controlled facility and driven only occasionally for maintenance and enjoyment. It represents an excellent opportunity to acquire a well-preserved example of automotive history.
